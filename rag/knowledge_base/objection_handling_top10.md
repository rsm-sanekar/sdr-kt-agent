# Top 10 cold-call objections (ARR pattern)

Every response below follows the **Acknowledge — Reframe — Redirect** pattern, internally referred to as **ARR**. ARR is required phrasing in Coaching Rubric v3.1: an objection answered without an explicit acknowledgement loses 1–2 points on dimension 3. Author: **Priya Subramanian**.

## The ten objections, in frequency order

### 1. "We're not in the market right now."
- **Acknowledge:** "Totally fair — most of the leaders we talk to aren't actively shopping."
- **Reframe:** "The reason I called is to share what peers in your space are doing about [specific trend], not to pitch anything today."
- **Redirect:** "Worth a 15-minute call next Tuesday or Thursday?"

### 2. "We already use [competitor]."
- **Acknowledge** the incumbent. **Reframe** to a workflow or data gap. **Redirect** to a peer reference call. Do *not* compare features directly without legal review.

### 3. "Send me an email."
- **Acknowledge.** **Reframe** by asking one qualifying question that determines what to include. **Redirect** by confirming you will follow up to book time.

### 4. "We don't have budget."
- **Acknowledge.** **Reframe:** "Budget cycles vary; we typically start the conversation 4–6 months before procurement." **Redirect** to a future-dated discovery call.

### 5. "Now is not a good time."
- **Acknowledge.** **Reframe:** "Quick question while I have you — are you the right person for [topic]?" **Redirect** to rescheduling specifically.

### 6. "We're going to build it ourselves."
- **Acknowledge** the engineering ambition. **Reframe** to total cost of ownership. **Redirect** to a build-vs-buy worksheet (internal asset BVB-2024-FSC).

### 7. "We're locked in a multi-year contract."
- **Acknowledge.** **Reframe** to readiness for renewal cycle. **Redirect** to a strategic check-in 9 months pre-renewal.

### 8. "I'm not the decision-maker."
- **Acknowledge.** **Reframe** by asking who is. **Redirect** by asking for a warm intro, not a cold name.

### 9. "Your product is too expensive."
- **Acknowledge.** **Reframe** to outcomes per dollar. **Redirect** to an ROI conversation with an SE, not a price negotiation.

### 10. "We had a bad experience with Salesforce before."
- **Acknowledge** specifically (don't dismiss). **Reframe** to what's changed (a recent product launch, a new CSM model). **Redirect** to a no-strings 20-minute call with the AE.
