# SDR Certification Question Bank

The five questions an SDR must answer during certification (Decision Gate 1), one per
rubric dimension in `certification_rubric.md`. The trainer scores the candidate's answers
against the rubric; the AI gap analysis cites these questions when surfacing evidence.

If a candidate's answers are missing, weak, or off-topic for any of these questions, the
gap-analysis output will flag the corresponding dimension as failing.

---

## Q1 — Product knowledge accuracy
Name and briefly describe the core Salesforce clouds (Sales Cloud, Service Cloud,
Marketing Cloud, Data Cloud, and the Agentforce AI layer). For each, state the business
outcome it enables in plain language. Do not list features — describe what the customer
gets done.

---

## Q2 — Objection handling (Defuse, Discover, Deliver)
Walk through how you would handle a price objection on a cold call using the three-step
Defuse / Discover / Deliver method. Include the specific clarifying question you would
ask in the Discover step, and how you would reframe the concern in the Deliver step.

---

## Q3 — MEDDIC framework application
Imagine this scenario: a prospect has been responsive over three calls and seems
genuinely interested. They are a Director of Operations. You have not spoken to their VP,
their procurement team, or their finance lead. They have asked you to send a proposal.

Walk through MEDDIC for this opportunity. Identify which elements are captured and which
are missing. Would you call this a qualified opportunity ready for an AE handoff, or
not? Justify your answer.

---

## Q4 — Salesforce value messaging
Pitch Sales Cloud to a VP of Sales at a 500-person SaaS company. Lead with the business
outcome they care about, not features. Quantify the value where you can. Avoid claims
you cannot back to a published source.

---

## Q5 — Discovery and questioning
Write five open-ended discovery questions you would ask in the first ten minutes of a
cold call with a Director of Operations at a regional healthcare provider. Each
question should surface either pain, priority, or context. Avoid yes/no questions and
avoid leading the witness.
