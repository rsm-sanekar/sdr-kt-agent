# Cold-call coaching rubric (v3.1)

Coaching Rubric v3.1 is the internal scoring sheet managers and Trail Guides use during weekly call reviews. Every recorded cold call sampled by Gong is scored on a 1-10 scale across four dimensions, then collapsed to a single weighted overall score. This document defines what each band means and where points are commonly lost.

## The 1-10 scale

| Score | Meaning | Typical outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 9–10 | Exemplary. Used as a training clip. | Meeting booked with senior decision-maker; clean recap. |
| 7–8 | Strong. Minor refinements only. | Meeting booked; rep self-aware about gaps. |
| 5–6 | Developing. Coaching plan recommended. | Mixed outcome; some discovery achieved. |
| 3–4 | Below bar. Two-week coaching cadence required. | No meeting; weak discovery; objections mishandled. |
| 1–2 | Unacceptable. Manager intervention same week. | Compliance issue, brand-damaging language, or call abandoned. |

## The four scored dimensions

Each dimension is scored 1-10 and the overall is a weighted average using the weights below.

1. **Opening & framing** (weight 0.20) — does the rep state who they are, why they're calling, and earn 30 more seconds within the first 15?
2. **Discovery quality** (weight 0.35) — how many qualified discovery questions, and how well did the rep follow up on prospect answers?
3. **Objection handling** (weight 0.25) — did the rep use the Acknowledge–Reframe–Redirect pattern (see `objection_handling_top10.md`)?
4. **Close & next step** (weight 0.20) — was a specific, calendared next step proposed and accepted?

## Common deductions (do not skip — these are checked every review)

- **Talking past the prospect** (–1 to –2): rep keeps talking when the prospect tries to interject.
- **Pitch dumping** (–2): rep launches into product features before any discovery.
- **Unapproved claims** (–3 and escalation): any forward-looking statement, revenue claim, or named-competitor comparison without legal approval per LMG-2024.
- **Skipping the recap** (–1): no verbal recap of next steps before hangup.

Scores below 5 are reviewed jointly by the SDR's manager and **Priya Subramanian** within five business days.
