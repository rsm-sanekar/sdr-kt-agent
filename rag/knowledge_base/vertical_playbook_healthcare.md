# Healthcare playbook — "Project Stethoscope"

The internal codename for our healthcare GTM motion is **Project Stethoscope**. This document gives SDRs the talking points, objection handlers, and competitive positioning needed to qualify a healthcare opportunity into a discovery call.

## HIPAA compliance talking points

Healthcare prospects screen for HIPAA fluency in the first 90 seconds. SDRs must be able to say, accurately and without legal review:

- Salesforce Health Cloud is built on the HIPAA-compliant Hyperforce infrastructure; a signed BAA is included as standard at the Enterprise tier and above.
- PHI is encrypted at rest with FIPS-140-2 validated modules and in transit with TLS 1.3.
- Access logs are immutable for 7 years and exportable for audit on request.
- The most recent third-party HIPAA attestation is dated **March 2024** and available under NDA.

**Do not** speculate about HITRUST status or specific state privacy laws (CMIA, MHMDA) without looping in Legal — see `legal_messaging_constraints.md`.

## Common buyer objections

| Objection | Approved short response | Escalation if pressed |
|---|---|---|
| "We're locked into Epic / Cerner workflows" | Acknowledge; pivot to the Workflow Connector that surfaces Salesforce data inside the EHR's right-rail. | Loop in the Health Cloud SE. |
| "We can't move PHI to the cloud" | Acknowledge; note Hyperforce Sovereign and the optional on-prem edge cache. | Loop in Hyperforce specialist. |
| "We already use Veeva for life sciences" | Clarify their primary use case — Veeva is strong in pharma CRM; we win in provider, payer, and patient engagement. | See competitive section below. |

## Competitive differentiators vs. Veeva

The single most important talking point: **Veeva is purpose-built for pharma reps calling on HCPs; Salesforce Health Cloud is purpose-built for provider, payer, and patient-360 use cases.** Do not disparage Veeva — many of our largest healthcare accounts use both.

Three differentiators sanctioned by Marketing for SDR use:

1. Patient-360 view including consented social and device data
2. Native Service Cloud integration for call-center and member-services teams
3. Einstein-powered next-best-action specifically trained on payer and provider workflows

Anything beyond these three requires AE or SE involvement. Project Stethoscope SE roster is maintained by Dr. Anita Bhatt.
