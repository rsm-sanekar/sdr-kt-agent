# SDR quota ramp schedule (FY24-Q2)

Ramp Schedule FY24-Q2 is the binding ramp curve for any SDR whose start date falls between **April 1, 2024 and March 31, 2025**. Hires starting later transition to FY25-Q1 once published. Owner: **Ravi Anand, Director of Sales Operations**.

## Standard ramp (entry-level hires)

The standard ramp applies to entry-level SDR hires (0–1 years prior sales experience). Quota attainment expectations are expressed as a percentage of the rep's full annualized quota:

| Period | Quota expectation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | 25% of full quota | Pure ramp; focus on activity metrics, not bookings. |
| Month 2 | 60% of full quota | First "real" attainment month; partial commission. |
| Month 3 | 100% of full quota | Full quota in effect; full commission plan. |

Commission for months 1 and 2 is paid against a guaranteed minimum equal to 70% of target variable. Month 3 onward is fully variable.

## Mid-level ramp (2–4 years prior sales)

Mid-level hires ramp on a compressed schedule:

- Month 1: 50% of full quota.
- Month 2: 90% of full quota.
- Month 3 onward: 100%.

Guaranteed minimum applies in month 1 only.

## Senior ramp (5+ years prior sales)

Senior hires (including hires with prior Salesforce experience) go to 100% quota on **Day 30**. No guaranteed minimum past Day 30 unless approved as a hiring concession during offer negotiation.

## Quota relief triggers

Attainment is adjusted, not waived, in the following internally documented cases:

1. **Territory split** mid-ramp: prorated relief, calculated by Ravi Anand's team.
2. **PIP exit and re-territory:** the incoming rep inherits the outgoing rep's ramp position, not a fresh start.
3. **Mandatory training holds** (e.g., a recall on a vertical playbook): days held are added to the ramp calendar.

## Activity targets during ramp

Quota is the dollar outcome; activity targets are the leading indicators. During Month 1 every SDR is held to: 60 outbound dials/day, 25 personalized emails/day, 5 LinkedIn touches/day, 3 booked discovery calls/week.
