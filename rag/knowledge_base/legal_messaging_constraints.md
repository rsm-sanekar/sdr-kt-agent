# Legal messaging constraints (LMG-2024)

The **Legal Approval Workflow LMG-2024** governs every claim an SDR makes in writing or on a call. The intent is not to slow down outreach — it is to keep individual reps from triggering regulatory inquiries or competitor litigation by improvising. This document is a quick reference; the full workflow lives in the Legal portal. Owner: **Sasha Lindgren, Sales Counsel**.

## What SDRs are NOT allowed to say

### No specific revenue or financial claims without legal review
Examples that are *not* allowed in outbound:

- "We grew ARR 40% last quarter."
- "Customers see an average 3.2x ROI in year one."
- "We saved [named customer] $4M."

Any specific number — percentage, dollar, multiplier, count — must come from a Legal-approved asset (case study, press release, 10-Q). Approved case-study quotes carry an internal code (e.g., `CSQ-2024-117`) that you can cite by reference.

### No comparative claims about named competitors
Do not say or write any of the following without LMG-2024 approval:

- "Veeva can't do X."
- "We're faster than [competitor]."
- "[Competitor]'s SOC 2 doesn't cover Y."

Comparing to a *named* competitor invites tortious-interference and false-advertising claims. The approved pattern is to describe Salesforce's capability and let the prospect draw the comparison.

### No forward-looking statements
SDRs cannot speculate on:

- Roadmap timelines ("Feature Z ships in Q4")
- Pricing changes ("Prices are going up in January")
- Acquisitions, partnerships, or organizational changes ("We're acquiring …")
- Performance projections ("You'll see a 20% lift")

Forward-looking statements are Investor Relations territory and only IR-approved language can be repeated externally.

## How to get something approved

1. Draft the proposed language.
2. Submit through the LMG-2024 intake form on the Legal portal.
3. SLA: 1 business day for low-risk paraphrasing, 5 business days for new claims.
4. Approved language is published as a snippet in the Outreach snippet library, tagged `legal-approved` and dated.

## What to do if a prospect asks something you can't answer

The approved response is: *"Great question — let me bring in our subject-matter expert."* Then loop in the AE or SE. **Do not guess.** A guess that turns into a written statement triggers most LMG-2024 escalations.
