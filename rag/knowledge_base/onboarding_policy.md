# SDR onboarding policy (SDR-OB-2024-03)

This policy defines the required pre-boarding and first-30-day activities for every new Salesforce SDR hire. It supersedes SDR-OB-2023-11 and is binding for all global SDR hiring managers. Questions about exceptions are routed to **Maya Reyes in People Ops** (maya.reyes@salesforce.example, ext. 4188).

## Required Trailhead modules by experience level

Every new hire must complete the modules below before their first live dial. Module completion is tracked automatically through Trailhead and surfaced on the manager's Day-15 dashboard.

| Experience level | Required modules | Target completion |
|---|---|---|
| Entry (0–1 yrs prior sales) | SDR Fundamentals, Prospecting 101, Salesforce CRM Basics, Outreach Foundations, Discovery Calls I | End of Week 2 |
| Mid (2–4 yrs prior sales) | Prospecting 201, Multi-Threading, Objection Handling Patterns, Industry Verticals Overview | End of Week 1 |
| Senior (5+ yrs prior sales) | Strategic Account Mapping, Enterprise Discovery, Competitive Landscape Briefing | End of Week 1 |

Managers are responsible for granting attainment credit; People Ops audits attainment on the 30th of each month.

## Mandatory shadowing requirements

Shadowing is non-negotiable. Hires are *not* permitted on live discovery calls or to send outbound sequences from their own seat until shadowing is complete.

- **Entry hires:** 14 calendar days of shadowing assigned AEs and senior SDRs. Minimum 25 observed calls.
- **Mid hires:** 7 calendar days of shadowing. Minimum 12 observed calls.
- **Senior hires:** 3 calendar days of shadowing. Minimum 5 observed calls plus one win-room participation.

Shadowing logs are submitted via the SDR Onboarding Workspace and reviewed by the hire's Trail Guide mentor at the end of the shadow window.

## Exceptions and waivers

Any deviation from this policy — including shortening the shadow window for a senior hire with prior Salesforce experience — requires written approval from the regional SDR Director **and** Maya Reyes. Verbal approvals are not valid. Waiver requests are logged in ticket queue `OB-WAIVER`.
