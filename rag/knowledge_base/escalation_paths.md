# Onboarding escalation paths

When something goes wrong during onboarding, the wrong escalation costs more time than the original problem. This doc maps common failure modes to the correct owner and channel. Owner: **Trevor Ellison, Onboarding Operations**.

## Day-1 IT problems

If a hire's laptop, badge, SSO, or Day-1 tooling is not working on the morning of Day 1:

1. The hire opens a **ServiceDesk ticket** (kiosk in the lobby, or servicedesk.salesforce.example for remote). Severity: "Onboarding-blocking".
2. SLA on onboarding-blocking tickets is **2 hours** during business hours.
3. The hiring manager is auto-notified — no parallel ticket needed.
4. If unacknowledged in 30 minutes, the manager pings Linh Truong in Slack `#it-service-desk`.

Do not skip the ticket. Slack-only escalations get lost.

## Mentor conflicts

If the Trail Guide pairing is not working:

- **First step:** the mentee tells their SDR manager. The manager has a one-on-one with the mentor.
- **Second step (if unresolved within one week):** the manager files a re-pairing request with **Devon Park, Mentor Program Lead** (see `mentor_program_handbook.md`).
- **Re-pairing is not punitive.** Devon's team tracks fit outcomes; about 8% of pairings re-pair in the first 30 days, and most are pure schedule-mismatch.

## Quota concerns

If a new hire feels the ramp curve is unrealistic for their territory or vertical:

1. Discuss with the SDR manager in the regular 1:1. The manager has discretion to revise activity targets within the ramp window but **cannot** revise the dollar quota.
2. If unresolved, escalate to **Maya Reyes in People Ops** (cc'd by the manager). Maya partners with Ravi Anand in Sales Ops to validate the territory load.
3. Formal ramp-relief requests are submitted to the comp committee on the 15th of each month.

## Crisis line

For after-hours emergencies — personal safety, severe harassment, suspected fraud or data breach — the crisis line is **x4427** (internal). The line is staffed 24/7 by People Ops on-call and routes to the right specialist (Security, ER, or Legal) within five minutes. Do not use the crisis line for technical or scheduling problems.

## Anonymous channel

For concerns the hire prefers to raise anonymously, the EthicsPoint portal is the sanctioned route, triaged by the Office of the General Counsel within two business days.
