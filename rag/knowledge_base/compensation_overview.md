# SDR compensation overview — FY2024

**Confidential — Internal Only.** Do not share outside Salesforce. Numbers are USD-equivalent target levels for FY2024 and are subject to annual recalibration. Comp questions go to **Janelle Foster** in Total Rewards.

## Base salary bands by territory and level

Bands reflect mid-points; offer ranges land within ±10% of midpoint depending on years of prior sales experience and local cost-of-labor differentials.

| Territory | Entry SDR base | Mid SDR base | Senior SDR base |
|---|---|---|---|
| AMER-West | $65,000 | $78,000 | $92,000 |
| AMER-East | $61,000 | $74,000 | $88,000 |
| EMEA (UK/DE/FR norm) | £48,000 | £58,000 | £70,000 |
| APAC (Singapore norm) | S$72,000 | S$88,000 | S$104,000 |

India (Bengaluru) and Brazil (São Paulo) have separate locality tables maintained by Janelle's team; ask before quoting numbers from those geos.

## Variable / commission structure

Target variable is layered on top of base. The full year on-target earnings (OTE) for an entry SDR in AMER-West is **$65K base + $25K variable = $90K OTE**. Other bands follow the same proportional split (roughly 72% base / 28% variable).

Commission is paid monthly with a one-month lag and is composed of:

1. **Meeting-set commission** — $150 per qualified discovery meeting that converts to Stage-2 pipeline. 60% of variable.
2. **Pipeline-sourced ARR commission** — accelerator of 6% on Stage-4+ ARR sourced from the SDR's accepted meetings. 30% of variable.
3. **Team kicker** — 10% of variable, paid when the regional pod hits 100% of team quota for the quarter.

## OTE accelerators

Above 100% attainment, the meeting-set commission accelerates: 1.25× from 100–120% and 1.5× from 120%+. Accelerators are paid in the quarter earned, not retroactively.

## SPIFFs

Cross-sell SPIFFs are published quarterly by Janelle and are *not* included in OTE. The Q3-FY24 SPIFF is $500 per qualified Data Cloud meeting that converts to Stage-3 within 30 days.

## Equity

SDRs receive RSUs vesting on the standard 1-year cliff / 4-year monthly schedule. Grant sizes are role-banded and published in the offer packet.
