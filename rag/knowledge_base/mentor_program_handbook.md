# Trail Guide mentor program handbook (TG Program v4.2)

The Trail Guide program pairs every new SDR hire with a tenured AE — their "Trail Guide" — for the first 90 days. Version 4.2 is in effect as of September 2024 and replaces v4.1. Program contact: **Devon Park, Mentor Program Lead** (devon.park@salesforce.example, Slack `@devonp`).

## Mentor-mentee meeting cadence

Cadence is enforced through calendar invites generated automatically when the pairing is confirmed in the Trail Guide Workspace.

- **Weeks 1–2:** 30-minute syncs every business day. Topic: shadowing debriefs and territory orientation.
- **Weeks 3–6:** Two 45-minute syncs per week. Topic: live-call coaching and pipeline hygiene.
- **Weeks 7–12:** One 60-minute sync per week plus one ride-along per fortnight on a live AE discovery call.
- **Post-90-day:** Optional monthly check-ins; many pairings continue informally.

Missed syncs must be rescheduled within 48 hours. Three missed-and-not-rescheduled syncs trigger an automatic escalation to Devon Park.

## When to escalate

The Trail Guide is the first line of support, **not** the only line. Escalate when:

1. The mentee is on track for a missed Week-4 readiness review (escalate to the SDR manager).
2. The mentor-mentee fit is poor for non-coaching reasons — schedule conflict, communication style mismatch, interpersonal friction — escalate to Devon Park. Re-pairing requests are processed within 5 business days.
3. The mentee discloses a personal hardship affecting their work. Route to People Ops (Maya Reyes), not through the mentor.
4. The mentor identifies a compliance or messaging concern. Route to Legal/Marketing per LMG-2024.

## Mentor satisfaction surveys

Mentors complete a short pulse survey at Days 14, 45, and 90. The Day-90 survey feeds into the AE's "Internal Citizenship" performance dimension and counts toward the annual "Trail Guide of the Year" award (announced at the Q4 SDR offsite).

## Trail Guide eligibility

AEs become eligible after 4 quarters of attainment ≥ 90% and one full sales cycle led. Devon Park maintains the eligibility roster, which is refreshed on the first Monday of each quarter.
